      AdaptedJoinableTreeExtensions Class

      Utility methods for converting between different "views" of the same joinable tree. The conversions are O(1) and the converted collection operates on the same underlying tree as the original collection.

      Note that the methods are also applicable to JoinableTreeNodeT.
